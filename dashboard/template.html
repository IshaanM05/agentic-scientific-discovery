Round 1

    Every hypothesis states a measurable prediction and the result that would kill it. Open ones are ranked by pairwise Elo matches judged on goal relevance, information value and novelty; hypotheses whose region cannot meet spec are parked.

    A film counts as a discovery only after the Judge sees it meet spec in two independent syntheses.

    Each film takes a day or more. While both synthesis stations are busy, the analysis agents keep refitting models, re-ranking hypotheses and re-reading literature.
