# Judge Q&A prep: 15 hard questions

Short honest answers drawn from the repo. TO FILL in square brackets marks a number that needs live Omnigent runs not yet in the repo.

1. **Could the model have memorised the steel data?** Possibly; the probe flag is SET (guided MAE 189 vs generic 255 MPa; 3/20 near-exact), though the probe is weak (n=20, default sampling). So we make no claim that LLM knowledge accelerates discovery (`knowledge/concepts/memorization-control.md`).
2. **What did you do about it?** A blinded arm (names hidden, features permuted and scaled) still gave 8.75 hits@60 vs BO 6.85 (CI [+0.90, +2.95], 20 seeds); a pre-registered Ni/Mn label swap met its rule (8.10; vs BO +1.25, CI [+0.20, +2.30]) but is a weak manipulation; and LabLoop gives unseen worlds.
3. **LabLoop is team-designed. Why trust it?** Don't, for real devices: it is a benchmark. Its value is that worlds are unseen, so memorisation cannot explain a gain. We reran its benchmark (`docs/RESULTS_LABLOOP.md`, 20 unseen worlds, budget 60): 9.9 vs 8.25 mean hits at 60 units for LabLoop vs BO, paired +1.65 [+0.05, +3.20], barely above zero; median first hit 6.5 vs 30.5 units. On 60 further unseen worlds x 20 seeds (`docs/RESULTS_LABLOOP_BIG.md`, bootstrap over worlds): 9.86 [8.88, 10.89] vs 8.04 [7.43, 8.67], paired +1.82 [+1.29, +2.35]; median first hit 7.0 vs 30.0 units. These numbers come from the rule-based LabLoop scientist, not the Omnigent runs.
4. **Is the approval gate a hard block?** In interactive runs yes: the call was held 22.2 s until a human clicked Approve (`runs/t011-repl30`). Headless `-p` runs decline automatically (fail-closed). Shown in one interactive run; replication still needed.
5. **Why is the hits-within-budget gain small?** About +1.9 hits at 60 experiments vs BO; the CI excludes 0 but is wide, the benchmark has only 15 hits, and BO wins 2 of 20 seeds. We report it as evidence on a benchmark, not as 10x.
6. **Did the agents actually get faster?** Not shown. Our own rule for an acceleration claim is not met (memorisation flag), and we did not time wall-clock or benchmark parallel speedup. Experiments to first/3rd/5th hit were lower than baselines (blind 11.1/18.4/27.4 vs BO 18.0/25.4/42.2).
7. **What would real validation need?** A post-cutoff or private dataset, temperature-0 or multi-sample probes, more seeds, a real or higher-fidelity oracle, expert review of recommendations and safety, and an independent replication of the approval hold.
8. **What failed and what did you change?** The live e2 run had 0 hits in 4 and no ADAPT line, so the ADAPT example comes from `runs/t011`; the first pick in `runs/live2` was tainted by a prompt example and is not used; the first arena submit failed the schema and was resubmitted; the trust meter did not detect the misleading swapped prior. We report these in the README.
9. **Does the planner really adapt after a result?** Yes in `runs/t011`: H2 refuted (1123.1 vs 2300 MPa predicted), insight wrote H3, the next experiment changed. Not in `runs/e2-live`. LabLoop: the rule-based baseline logs ADAPT events (mean 6.2, 11.6, 8.0 on worlds 2000, 2001, 2002, 5 seeds each; `runs/ll_compare.json`); Omnigent ADAPT events: world 2000 partial live run `runs/ll-w2000`: 5 by `ll_compare`'s count (the replay dashboard's stricter inferred count is 2; the planner wrote no "ADAPT:" line in it); the 8-unit smoke run `runs/ll-smoke2000` has 1 planner ADAPT line; worlds 2001 and 2002 **[TO FILL: not run]**.
10. **Is the judge or the arena evidence of skill?** No. On steel (small n): the judge was 12/12 but sees the ledger value, so near-arithmetic; the arena's Spearman 0.56 (p=0.21) is chance-level. On LabLoop (larger n, team-designed simulator): judge precision 0.96, recall 0.58 over 579 films; arena Spearman 0.39 [0.28, 0.50] over 218 hypotheses. Both stay advisory, not gates.
11. **How good is the safety agent?** The safety gate is a keyword list over tool arguments, not a hazard analysis; an unlisted hazard is not blocked. Experiments here are replayed data, so nothing certifies lab safety.
12. **Why does this need Omnigent rather than a Python loop?** The brief requires it. Here Omnigent runs the specialists as live sessions, enforces policies before tools run, and records sessions; the oracle and selector are function tools.
13. **How novel are your hypotheses?** Unknown. The novelty check is a shallow OpenAlex keyword search (1 "already reported", 4 "partly reported", 0 "no match found"); it is not proof of novelty, and all hypotheses are agent-generated.
14. **How do you differ from SciAgents, Coscientist, A-Lab?** SciAgents (as described in its method) generates and critiques hypotheses without running experiments; ours runs the experiment and lets the result change the next decision. We did not reproduce SciAgents, and Coscientist and A-Lab do run experiments, in real labs; we do not.
15. **What would it take to approach 10x?** Not claimed. Our proposal, untested: a genuinely unseen dataset, a stronger surrogate and prior, per-step re-querying of the prior (ours is static), and parallel experiment batches; each needs its own measurement.
